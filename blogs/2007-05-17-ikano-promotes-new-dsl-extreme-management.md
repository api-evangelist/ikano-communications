IKANO Communications, Inc., a leading provider of private-label, wholesale and retail Internet services, has promoted new management at its Los Angeles-based DSLExtreme operations. George Mitsopoulos was named Vice President and General Manager of DSLExtreme and assumes responsibility for all day-to-day operations. Dean Russ will serve as Vice President and Chief Technical Officer of DSLExtreme
